Thesis

Autonomy will reach industry as an upgrade, not a replacement.

Most of the money in robotics is chasing new machines. Humanoids, robotaxis and robot foundation models drew billions in 2025. Those bets need years of capital before a customer pays for a single shift.

The machines that run the economy are already built. Farmers own 3.8 million tractors, and 88% were made before 2018. Plants keep a CNC cell for decades. Operators will not scrap equipment that works. They will pay to make it run without a full crew. We back the companies that sell them that upgrade.

Three convictions

What we believe that most of the market does not.

i.

The installed base is the market.

Every tractor, forklift, excavator and inspection line already in service is a buyer for autonomy. The customer has a budget, a measured labor cost and a machine that will run for another decade.

ii.

Retrofit beats rebuild.

A kit and a software subscription sold through the dealers and OEMs that already own the customer can reach revenue on a few million dollars. A new autonomous vehicle needs hundreds of millions first.

We fund the first kind of company. That is how a seed fund owns a real share of the outcome.

iii.

Proximity wins the deal and the deployment.

The Midwest produces about 16% of US GDP and receives roughly 3% to 5% of US venture capital. Industrial buyers adopt from people who show up at the plant. We live a drive away from them, and so do the founders we back.

Source: Dealroom and StartMidwest, Mapping the Midwest Innovation Ecosystem (April 2026).
Why now

Three curves crossed at once.

Labor

The crews are not coming back.

Plants, farms and warehouses cannot staff every shift. Buyers now price autonomy as shift coverage with a payback date.

Hardware

Sensing got cheap.

Cameras, lidar and edge compute cost a fraction of what they did a few years ago. A retrofit kit now pencils out on a single machine.

Models

Autonomy ships as software.

Vision and planning models now generalize across sites and machines. Each deployment teaches the next one, and the data stays with the company.
